Preview meshes derived from dorianborian/sesame-robot
(https://github.com/dorianborian/sesame-robot).

- Source: hardware/cad/Sesame-ESP32-v122.step (Apache License 2.0).
- parts/*.stl are a tessellation of that STEP assembly (linear deflection
  0.35 mm), split by CAD colour and converted to metres. The colours are
  the diffuse colours from the STEP file.
- The repository does not publish joint frames, so the preview shows the
  assembled CAD pose rather than eight separate actuated joints.
- scene_viewer.xml adds the MuJoCo preview floor, sky, and light, and
  places the assembly on the floor.
